## Prepared remarks — CEO

Good evening, everyone. FY26 has started strongly. Revenue for the quarter was **₹1,240 crore, up 18% year on year**. We commissioned **320 MW** of solar and wind capacity during the quarter and our order book stands at a healthy **₹9,800 crore**, giving us roughly two years of revenue visibility.

Demand across our EPC and independent power producer (IPP) segments remains firm. State utilities continue to tender aggressively, and our win rate on competitive bids held above 30%. Execution was on track this quarter, with no major project slippages.

## Prepared remarks — CFO

On profitability, **EBITDA margin came in at 21.5%**, in line with our expectations, supported by stable module prices and disciplined project selection. **Profit after tax was ₹142 crore**. Net debt to EBITDA stood at **2.4 times**, comfortable for a company in our capital-intensive segment.

On guidance: for the full year FY26 we expect **revenue growth of 20–22%** and an **EBITDA margin of around 21%**. We will maintain our disciplined approach to bidding and will not chase volume at the cost of returns.

## Q&A

**Analyst (Priya, Kotak):** Congratulations on the start. How should we think about module cost trends for the rest of the year?

**CFO:** Module prices were stable this quarter. We have contracted a large share of our near-term module requirement, so we are reasonably hedged for the next two quarters. If prices rise later in the year, there could be some margin pressure, but we do not see that today.

**Analyst (Rohan, Jefferies):** Any risks on the receivables side from state discoms?

**CFO:** Receivables are within normal range this quarter. Collections from state distribution companies have been timely. We watch this closely, as it is the single biggest working-capital swing factor in our business, but there is nothing to flag in Q1.

**Analyst (Priya, Kotak):** And the order book — is the mix shifting?

**CEO:** The mix is tilting slightly more towards IPP, which is margin-accretive over the asset life but ties up capital longer. We are comfortable with the balance. We expect order book to keep growing through the year.

Thank you all. We look forward to updating you next quarter.

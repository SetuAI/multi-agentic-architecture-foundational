## Prepared remarks — CEO

We closed FY26 on a strong note and recovered well from a difficult Q3. Revenue for Q4 was **₹1,520 crore, up 22% year on year** and our best quarter ever. We commissioned **410 MW**, including **120 MW of the previously delayed 200 MW project** — the transmission approval came through and the remaining 80 MW is scheduled for early FY27.

On the receivable I flagged last quarter: of the **₹340 crore overdue from the state discom, we have recovered ₹210 crore**, and we expect the balance over the next two quarters. Working capital has eased as a result.

Order book reached a **record ₹12,100 crore**. We also announced a **module manufacturing joint venture under the government's production-linked incentive (PLI) scheme**, which over time reduces our dependence on imported modules and helps insulate margins.

## Prepared remarks — CFO

**EBITDA margin recovered to 20.9%**, back near our normal range, as delayed revenue was recognised and one-off costs rolled off. **Profit after tax was ₹176 crore**, a record. Net debt to EBITDA improved to **2.5 times** as the receivable recovery reduced borrowing.

For full-year FY26, revenue was approximately **₹5,250 crore**. Looking ahead, we are **guiding FY27 revenue growth of 22–25%** and an **EBITDA margin of around 21–22%**, helped by a strong order book and, later in the year, the manufacturing JV.

## Q&A

**Analyst (Priya, Kotak):** The FY27 guidance is a big step up. What underpins it?

**CEO:** Three things: the record order book, the delayed FY26 revenue that spills into FY27, and easing input costs as our own module supply comes on stream. We are confident, though execution on grid connectivity remains the thing to watch.

**Analyst (Meera, Nuvama):** Does the manufacturing JV change your capital allocation?

**CFO:** It requires investment, yes, but it is staged and partly PLI-supported. We do not expect net debt to EBITDA to breach 3 times. It is a margin-defensive move more than a growth bet.

**Analyst (Rohan, Jefferies):** Any residual risk from the ALMM and duty changes you flagged in Q3?

**CFO:** The regulatory changes are now clearer and largely manageable. Our own manufacturing reduces the exposure over time. Discom collections remain our main external monitorable, but the situation has improved from Q3.

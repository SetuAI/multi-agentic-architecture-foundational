## Prepared remarks — CEO

Thank you for joining. Revenue for Q2 was **₹1,310 crore, up 16% year on year**. We commissioned **260 MW** this quarter — a little lower than Q1, mainly because monsoon conditions in two of our western project sites pushed some commissioning into October. Those megawatts are not lost; they simply shift into Q3.

Our order book grew to **₹10,400 crore**. Tendering activity remains strong and we added two new EPC contracts during the quarter.

## Prepared remarks — CFO

Profitability is where I want to spend a moment. **EBITDA margin was 19.8% this quarter, down from 21.5% in Q1.** Two factors drove this: first, imported solar module prices rose during the quarter and part of our Q2 execution used modules bought at the higher price; second, the monsoon-related commissioning delays meant we carried some fixed site costs without matching revenue recognition. **Profit after tax was ₹128 crore.** Net debt to EBITDA ticked up to **2.6 times**.

On guidance: we are **maintaining our full-year FY26 outlook of 20–22% revenue growth**, but we now expect **full-year EBITDA margin closer to 20–21%** rather than the earlier 21%, reflecting the input-cost environment. We believe the module price increase is partly transient.

## Q&A

**Analyst (Rohan, Jefferies):** The margin dip — how much is one-off versus structural?

**CFO:** I would say the monsoon-related portion, perhaps 60–70 basis points, is one-off and reverses in Q3. The module-price portion is harder to call; it depends on global supply. We have contracted forward where we could, but not fully.

**Analyst (Meera, Nuvama):** Any change in discom collection behaviour?

**CFO:** Collections were broadly normal this quarter, though one state was slower than usual. It is not yet a concern, but it is on our watch list. Working capital rose modestly as a result.

**Analyst (Rohan, Jefferies):** You maintained revenue guidance despite the delays — what gives you confidence?

**CEO:** The delayed megawatts commission in Q3, and our order book gives strong visibility. We are confident on the top line. Margin is the variable we are managing most actively right now.

Thank you. We will speak again after Q3.
